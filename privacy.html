Privacy policy

GhostHunt by Jim Kesselring · Effective October 12, 2026

GhostHunt is operated by James Kesselring. Contact: razoreqx@gmail.com.

Camera and augmented reality

The game requests camera permission to display your surroundings and use Apple's ARKit tracking to place fictional clues and equipment. The game's camera and AR processing take place on your device. The game does not upload camera frames, recordings of your surroundings, detected surfaces, or investigation positions to us.

Microphone and location

The EVP recorder plays simulated game audio; it does not record the microphone. Movement readings use AR device tracking, not GPS. The game does not request microphone or precise-location permission.

Saved progress

Hunter progress, earned currency, equipment, and capture records are saved locally on your device. We do not operate a cloud-save service or require a player account. Deleting the app may remove this data. No player database is maintained by us.

Optional Game Center leaderboard (version 1.0.5)

If you choose Join Game Center, the game sends your total successfully captured and turned-in ghosts to Apple's Game Center. Other players can see your Game Center nickname, score, and rank. Your camera images, evidence photos, room surveys, maps, GPS location, and support email are not sent with scores. We do not operate a leaderboard server or receive your Game Center credentials.

You can play without joining. Stop Sharing Scores disables further score submissions; previously submitted scores remain with Apple. Game Center account information and retained scores are managed by Apple under its Game Center privacy information. Your sharing preference and sound, vibration, and visual-effect settings are stored locally on your device.

Evidence photos, journal, and room surveys

If you use the in-game photo tool, evidence images of your surroundings are stored privately in the app on your device. The case journal retains up to 12 cases, with up to three photos per case; older case photos are pruned. Photos, confirmed readings, and case records are not automatically uploaded or shared. You can delete the evidence journal from the casebook after returning home. Copying a case report puts text on your clipboard; you choose whether and where to share it.

Observed surfaces and investigation positions are used for local gameplay mapping. On supported devices, the optional guided room survey uses Apple's RoomPlan. Survey data is session-local and is not uploaded to us.

Ads, purchases, and diagnostics

The current release contains no advertising or real-money purchases. Equipment uses earned game currency. Unity advertising, analytics, purchasing, and automatic engine/cloud diagnostic reporting are disabled in this release. We do not operate an in-game analytics or tracking service.

Apple may process App Store, TestFlight, and device diagnostic information under its own settings and policies. If you voluntarily send us a crash report or support message, we use the information you provide to investigate your request.

Support correspondence

If you email us, your email provider and ours process that correspondence. We receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to provide support, retain them only as needed for that purpose or legal obligations, and do not sell them. Email us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to required retention.

This website

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information, including IP addresses, under its privacy statement. These pages include no advertising, analytics scripts, or contact forms.

Changes and questions

Updates will be posted here with a revised effective date. Contact razoreqx@gmail.com with privacy questions. This horror game is not directed to children.
