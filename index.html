GhostHunt by Jim Kesselring

Support for the iPhone augmented-reality ghost-hunting game by James Kesselring.

Get help

Email razoreqx@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, game version, and what happened. Avoid including people or private details in screenshots.

Start an investigation

Allow camera access. In a clear, well-lit indoor area, slowly scan a floor surface and start the hunt. Follow detector activity from two separated locations, find two UV traces, then confirm a quiet EVP recording before deploying containment.

After capture, retrieve the floor containment device, return to the home office, and turn in the ghost for earned game currency and experience.

Troubleshooting

  • If placement fails, check camera permission in iPhone Settings, improve lighting, and aim at a clear floor.
  • If tracking is interrupted, pause and let the phone reacquire the surroundings before moving.
  • EVP is simulated and does not use your microphone. Record near activity while holding still.
  • The map records confirmed activity history; it does not scan a house floor plan or show the ghost's live position.
  • Progress is stored locally. Deleting the app can erase progress; do not reinstall unless you accept that risk.

Play safely

Move slowly. Keep real obstacles, stairs, roads, and other hazards out of your play area. Start sound low and stop if uncomfortable. All paranormal readings, ghosts, and clues are fictional effects.
